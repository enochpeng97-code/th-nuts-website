ABOUT THE COMPANY

公司介绍

Connecting nut origins, processing and international trade.

连接坚果产地、加工与国际贸易

Established in 2023, Guangzhou Tonghuan International Supply Chain Co., Ltd. is based in Guangzhou, China. Through TH Nuts, the company coordinates nut sourcing, processing and cross-border trade for professional food buyers.

广州市通寰国际供应链有限公司成立于2023年，立足中国广州。公司以 TH Nuts 品牌开展坚果采购、加工协同与跨境贸易，为专业食品采购商提供产品对接服务。

Our supply network includes macadamias sourced in Yunnan, raw in-shell cashews from West Africa shipped exclusively to Vietnam, and nuts processed in Vietnam for overseas markets. Vietnam-processed cashew kernels are also imported into China.

供应链涵盖云南夏威夷果采购、西非带壳生腰果原料专供越南，以及越南加工坚果销往海外市场；越南加工的腰果仁另有进口回中国的贸易路线。

We work with buyers to confirm product type, grade, packaging, volume and destination for each order. Common packaging is 20/30 kg cartons with individually vacuum-sealed inner bags, with an MOQ of 500 cartons. Typical lead time is within one month; FOB terms and payment timing are confirmed per order.

我们根据每笔订单与采购商确认产品类别、规格、包装、数量及目的地。常见包装为20/30公斤纸箱，内装独立抽真空密封袋，最小起订量500箱。常见交期一个月内，FOB及付款安排按订单确认。
